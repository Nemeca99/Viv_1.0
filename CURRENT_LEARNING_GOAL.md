learning in Viv's terminal while the deterministic WSL/Docker Body retains authority.

links, and unfinished learning survive the next session.

does not establish general grammar, factual truth, or spontaneous fluent speech.

process. A failed attempt is learning evidence, not grounds to erase progress.

new inputs. Do not substitute farm benchmarks or loss reduction for this objective.
